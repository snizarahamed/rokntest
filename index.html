About us

Securing spaces
since 2015

ROKN AL WAHA Technical Works LLC brings security systems and integrated technology together under one roof. As a SIRA-approved company in Dubai, we help businesses and property owners protect their assets, manage access, and keep essential systems connected through solutions tailored to their environments.

From CCTV surveillance and access control to alarm systems, PABX, audio-visual solutions, and smart-home automation, we manage the journey from equipment supply and installation to ongoing maintenance. With one team coordinating your technical requirements and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMCs) supporting system upkeep, you have a dependable partner to turn to beyond installation day.

Our approach is built on practical expertise, personalised service, and long-term client relationships—helping create safer spaces and more dependable day-to-day operations.
